No DRM. No watermark. Unsigned build: right-click the app and choose Open the first time.

Everything a wallpaper app should do

  • Discover

    Free wallpapers and looping videos from Wallhaven, Pexels, Bing, NASA and Pixabay. Safe-for-work filtering built in.

  • Library

    Drag in your own images and videos. Filter, sort, multi-select, rename, and preview clips on hover.

  • Live wallpapers

    Video plays behind your desktop icons, one light window per display, with a watchdog that revives a frozen clip.

  • Scenes

    Aurora, Nebula, Deep waves, Starfield, Drift and Night rain. Drawn on a canvas, so almost no storage.

  • Rotation

    Pick a set and an interval. Missing files are skipped, never fatal.

  • Music

    Paste a YouTube link or add audio files. The now-playing bar takes the colours of the video.

  • Displays

    Choose what each monitor shows.

  • Power-aware

    Pauses live wallpapers on battery, below a charge threshold, or in Low Power Mode. You set the policy.

  • Menu-bar panel

    Quick controls for wallpapers and music without opening the main window.
